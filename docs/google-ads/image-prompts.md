The images were generated with the built-in image generation tool as category-level Performance Max
creative. The prompt set covered these distinct scenes:

1. Complete business workstation with computer, printer, phone and networking equipment.
2. Modern video-conferencing meeting room.
3. Organised technology fulfilment workspace.
4. Business networking and communications installation.
5. Square business workstation composition.
6. Square video-conferencing composition.
7. Square office printing and scanning composition.
8. Square networking and communications composition.
9. Portrait business workstation composition.
10. Portrait video-conferencing composition.

Every prompt required premium photorealistic commercial photography, generic unbranded equipment,
important content within Google's center safe area, and no people, text, logos, prices, promotional
badges, watermarks, courier brands, or collage layouts.
